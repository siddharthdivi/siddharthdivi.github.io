Trip Date(s): August 15-17, 2025

## Trip Overview

A fantastic 3-day camping adventure at Four Bishops Campground in the Eastern Sierras near Bishop, California. This trip featured three incredible hikes: Blue Lake Trail, Big Pine Lakes Trail, and Little Lakes Valley Trail, offering diverse alpine experiences from serene lakes to dramatic mountain peaks.

## Campground Details

**Location**: [Four Bishops Campground](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233807?tab=info)  
**Elevation**: 8,100 feet  
**Amenities**: Picnic tables, food storage lockers, campfire rings with grills, accessible flush toilets, drinking water  
**Reservations**: Required via Recreation.gov  

## Daily Itinerary

### Day 1: Friday, August 15th
- **4:00 AM**: Depart from Bay Area
- **Morning**: Coffee/restroom stop en route
- **Lunch**: Pickup on the way
- **Hike**: [Lake Sabrina to Blue Lake Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/lake-sabrina-to-blue-lake-trail)
  - Distance: 5.9 miles
  - Elevation Gain: 1,453 feet
- **Evening**: Arrive at campground, setup, cook dinner

### Day 2: Saturday, August 16th
- **Morning**: Breakfast at camp
- **Hike**: [Big Pine Lakes Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/big-pine-lakes-trail)
  - Distance: 14.7 miles
  - Elevation Gain: 3,841 feet
- **Afternoon**: Optional post-hike relaxation at [Keough's Hot Springs](http://www.keoughshotsprings.com/)
  - Hours: 9 AM - 6 PM
  - Adult Day Pass: $14
- **Evening**: Dinner in town, return to camp

### Day 3: Sunday, August 17th
- **Morning**: Breakfast at camp, pack up
- **Lunch**: Pickup on the way
- **Hike**: [Little Lakes Valley to Gem Lakes](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/little-lakes-valley-to-gem-lakes)
  - Distance: 7.2 miles
  - Elevation Gain: 1,033 feet
- **Evening**: Dinner on the way back home

## Highlights

- **Three Epic Hikes**: Blue Lake, Big Pine Lakes, and Little Lakes Valley
- **High Elevation Camping**: 8,100 feet with stunning mountain views
- **Perseid Meteor Shower**: Perfect timing for stargazing (peak August 11-13)
- **Hot Springs**: Optional relaxation at Keough's Hot Springs
- **Diverse Terrain**: From alpine lakes to granite peaks
- **Wilderness Experience**: Remote camping with full amenities

## Trip Details

**Location**: Four Bishops Campground, Eastern Sierras, California  
**Duration**: 3 days, 2 nights  
**Activities**: Camping, hiking, photography, stargazing, hot springs  
**Best Time**: Summer months (June-September) for optimal weather

## Special Features

### Perseid Meteor Shower
- **Peak Dates**: August 11-13, 2025
- **Best Viewing**: Dark skies at 8,100 ft elevation
- **Timing**: First light 5:44 AM, Sunset 7:44 PM

### Hot Springs Option
- **Keough's Hot Springs**: Post-hike relaxation
- **Location**: 10 minutes from Bishop
- **Hours**: 9 AM - 6 PM (Saturday)
- **Cost**: $14 adult day pass

- **Dramatic Landscapes**: Steep mountain faces and high desert valleys
- **Unique Geology**: Volcanic formations and ancient rock layers
- **Diverse Ecosystems**: From high alpine to desert environments
- **Outdoor Recreation**: World-class hiking, climbing, and camping
- **Small Town Charm**: Bishop and other mountain communities
