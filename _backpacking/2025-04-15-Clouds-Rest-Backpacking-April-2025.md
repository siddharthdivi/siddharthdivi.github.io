Trip Date(s): April 15-17, 2025

## Trip Overview

A spectacular multi-day backpacking adventure to Clouds Rest, one of Yosemite's most iconic peaks. This trip offered stunning panoramic views of the Yosemite Valley, Half Dome, and the surrounding High Sierra wilderness.

## Highlights

- **Summit Views**: 360-degree panoramas from the Clouds Rest summit
- **Alpine Lakes**: Crystal clear mountain lakes perfect for camping
- **Wildflower Season**: Early spring blooms adding color to the landscape
- **Solitude**: Fewer crowds compared to summer months
- **Wildlife**: Opportunities to spot deer, marmots, and other alpine creatures

**Best Time**: Spring (April-May) for wildflowers, Fall for colors
