## Trip Overview

A perfect long weekend getaway to Michigan's Upper Peninsula during the July 4th holiday. This trip featured stunning Great Lakes shorelines, pristine forests, and charming small towns in one of America's most beautiful and underrated regions.

## Highlights

- **Great Lakes Shorelines**: Crystal clear waters and sandy beaches
- **Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore**: Dramatic cliffs and rock formations
- **Mackinac Island**: Historic island with no cars, only horses and bicycles
- **Tahquamenon Falls**: One of the largest waterfalls east of the Mississippi
- **Small Town Charm**: Quaint communities and local hospitality
- **Outdoor Adventures**: Hiking, kayaking, and scenic drives

## Trip Details

**Duration**: July 3-6, 2025 (4 days, 3 nights)  
**Location**: Michigan Upper Peninsula  
**Activities**: Sightseeing, hiking, kayaking, island exploration  
**Best Time**: Summer months (June-September) for optimal weather  
**Highlights**: Pictured Rocks, coastlines of the Great Lakes, Tahquamenon Falls  


## Michigan Upper Peninsula Features

The Upper Peninsula offers:
- **Pristine Wilderness**: Vast forests and untouched natural areas
- **Great Lakes Access**: Shorelines of Lake Superior, Lake Michigan, and Lake Huron
- **Unique Geology**: Pictured Rocks, waterfalls, and rock formations
- **Rich History**: Mining heritage, Native American culture, and maritime history
- **Outdoor Recreation**: World-class hiking, kayaking, and scenic drives
- **Small Town Life**: Charming communities with local character
